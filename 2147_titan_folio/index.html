About Me

Nathan Cattell is a senior public sector leader with extensive experience in executive governance, program delivery, and Aboriginal affairs.

Recognised for executive decision support, portfolio governance, financial stewardship, workforce leadership, and building high-trust partnerships with Aboriginal communities, councils, and state agencies to deliver regulatory, cultural, and environmental outcomes.

Leadership & Experience

Nathan is an Aboriginal man with expertise in Aboriginal governance, cultural heritage management, community engagement, and Closing the Gap program delivery. He has held senior roles in the NSW Environment Protection Authority and NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service, managing statewide programs, teams, and budgets.

His approach combines strategic planning, policy implementation, risk governance, and operational excellence to achieve measurable outcomes across communities and environmental projects.
